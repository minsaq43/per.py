Cron is a time‑based job scheduler for Unix‑like systems, created in 1975 at Bell Labs, that automatically runs commands or scripts at specified times using crontab schedule expressions.
What Cron Is
Cron is a daemon (crond) that checks every minute for scheduled tasks and executes those whose time fields match the current time.
The name comes from the Greek word chronos, meaning “time.”
